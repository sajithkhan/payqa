What to run

Test a checkout before it takes real money

PayQA runs your store against the PayPal sandbox and checks what happens when payments go wrong.

Chaos
Makes PayPal decline cards, refuse transactions and fail mid-capture, then checks your store didn't mark anything as paid.
Browser
Opens Chrome, shops like a customer, pays through the real PayPal popup, and compares what they saw with what PayPal charged.

Pick a store above (or enter its URL), choose a mode and press Run tests. To test your own store, add the PayQA Adapter to its staging server (see adapter/README.md).
